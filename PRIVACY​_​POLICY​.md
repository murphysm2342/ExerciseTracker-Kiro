# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 4, 2026**

## Overview

YMExercise Tracker ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind. Your workout data stays on your device.

## Data Collection

The App does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All workout data, user profiles, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage.

## HealthKit

The App may optionally read and write workout data from Apple Health (HealthKit). This data is only used locally to import or export your cardio workouts. HealthKit data is never sent to any server or third party.

## Watch Connectivity

If you use the Apple Watch companion app, workout data is transferred directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This communication happens locally between your paired devices.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Backup

The App offers an optional JSON export feature for personal backup purposes. Exported files are saved to a location you choose and are not transmitted to any server.
